Diagnostic Evaluation

The right evaluation for a clearer picture.

Appropriate diagnostic evaluation can help understand cardiovascular health, identify potential concerns and guide the next step in care.

01

ECG

Electrical activity of the heart evaluated as part of appropriate cardiac assessment.

02

Echocardiography

Imaging-based evaluation of heart structure and function when clinically indicated.

03

Stress Testing

Assessment of cardiovascular response to controlled physical stress when appropriate.

04

Holter Monitoring

Extended heart rhythm monitoring to help evaluate intermittent cardiac symptoms.

Specialized Care

Care that goes beyond the consultation.

01

Cardiology Consultation

A consultation provides an opportunity to understand your symptoms, medical history, risk factors and concerns before deciding on the appropriate next steps.

  • Detailed health discussion
  • Cardiovascular risk evaluation
  • Personalized recommendations
  • Follow-up planning when required
02

Heart Health Checkup

Preventive evaluation can help identify cardiovascular risk factors and create an informed plan for maintaining heart health.

  • Cardiovascular health review
  • Risk-factor assessment
  • Appropriate diagnostic evaluation
  • Preventive care guidance
03

Preventive Cardiology

Prevention focuses on understanding individual cardiovascular risks and building sustainable habits that support long-term heart health.

  • Risk reduction strategies
  • Lifestyle guidance
  • Regular monitoring
  • Long-term heart health planning
04

Hypertension Management

Blood pressure management is approached with attention to individual risk factors, health history and long-term cardiovascular protection.

  • Blood pressure assessment
  • Cardiovascular risk review
  • Personalized management plan
  • Ongoing monitoring
05

Cholesterol Management

Cholesterol-related cardiovascular risk is assessed in the context of your overall health rather than as an isolated number.

  • Lipid profile review
  • Cardiovascular risk assessment
  • Personalized recommendations
  • Follow-up monitoring
06

Cardiac Risk Assessment

A broader cardiovascular risk assessment helps bring together relevant health information to support prevention and informed clinical decisions.

  • Personal and family history review
  • Cardiovascular risk factors
  • Appropriate testing when indicated
  • Preventive recommendations
